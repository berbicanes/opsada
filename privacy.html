Opsada – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Opsada is a game. It does not ask for or sell personal information, and it shows no ads.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, cards, gold, settings and the player name you choose) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Online matches

When you play online, your device connects directly to your opponent's device (WebRTC). To set up the connection the app uses the public PeerJS server and STUN/TURN servers (Google, Metered). These services, and your opponent's device, can see your IP address while the match is being set up or relayed. The only game data sent is what the match needs: your player name, trophies, castle colour, chosen cards and your moves.

Clans and world leaderboard

When these features are on, the game creates an anonymous account (Google Firebase) and stores your player name, trophies, level, clan, the clan chat messages you write and your weekly wins in private leagues you join, so other players can see them. "Move progress to a new phone" uploads your progress under a one-time code that expires after 24 hours. No e-mail, phone number or real name is collected.

Children

In battles players can only send fixed emote phrases. Clan chat is limited to clan members. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer through the store listing.

Opsada – Politika privatnosti

Opsada ne traži i ne prodaje lične podatke i nema reklama. Klanovi i svjetska rang lista (kad su uključeni) koriste anonimni nalog (Google Firebase) i čuvaju ime, trofeje, klan, poruke iz klanskog chata i sedmične pobjede u ligama u koje uđeš. Napredak (nivoi, karte, zlato, postavke i ime koje izabereš) čuva se samo na tvom uređaju. U online meču tvoj uređaj se direktno spaja s uređajem protivnika (WebRTC); za uspostavu veze koriste se javni PeerJS server i STUN/TURN serveri (Google, Metered), koji kao i protivnik mogu vidjeti tvoju IP adresu. Šalje se samo ono što meč traži: ime, trofeji, boja zamka, karte i potezi.
